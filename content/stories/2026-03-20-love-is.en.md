In a foreign country, children aged 4-8 were asked, "What do you think love is?"

But their answers about love were much deeper and broader than what adults define.

"Love is when a girl puts on perfume and a boy puts on shaving cologneg and they go out and smell each other."

Love is when a girl puts on perfume, and a boy puts on aftershave, and they meet to smell each other. (Karl, age 5)

"Love is when someone hurts you. And you get so mad but you don't yell at them because you know it would hurt their feelings."

Love is when someone says something that hurts me or makes me angry, but I don't yell at them, because I know it would hurt their feelings. (Samantha, age 6)

"Love is what makes you smile when you're tired."

Love is what makes me smile when I'm tired. (Terri, age 4)

"Love is when my mommy makes coffee for my daddy and she takes a sip before giving it to him, to make sure the taste is OK."

Love is when mommy makes coffee for daddy and takes a sip before giving it to him, to make sure the taste is okay. (Danny, age 7)

"Love is when you kiss all the time. Then when you get tired of kissing, you still want to be together and you talk more.
My mommy and Daddy are like that. They look gross when they kiss."

Love is when you're always kissing. And even when you get tired of kissing, you still want to be together and want to talk. My mom and dad are like that. They look really gross when they kiss. (Emily, age 8)

"Love is when you tell a guy you like his shirt, then he wears it everyday."

Love is when you tell a boy you like his shirt, and then he wears that shirt every day. (Noelle, age 7)

"Love is like a little old woman and a little old man who are still friends even after they know each other so well."

Love is like an old woman and an old man who are still friends even after knowing so much about each other. (Tommy, age 6)

"During my piano recital, I was on a stage and scared.
I looked at all the people watching me and saw my daddy waving and smiling.
He was the only one doing that. I wasn't scared anymore."

During my piano recital, I was so nervous and scared. People in the audience were all looking at me, but I saw my daddy waving and smiling at me. He was the only one doing that. I wasn't scared anymore. (Cindy, age 8)
"Love is when mommy gives daddy the best piece of chicken."

Love is when mommy gives daddy the best piece of chicken. (Elaine, age 5)

"Love is when mommy sees daddy smelly and sweaty and still says he is handsomer than Robert Redford."

Love is when mommy says daddy is handsomer than Robert Redford, no matter how much he smells of sweat. (Chris, age 8)

"Love is when your puppy licks your face even after you left him alone all day."

Love is when my puppy licks my face, even though I left him alone at home all day. (Mary Ann, age 4)
