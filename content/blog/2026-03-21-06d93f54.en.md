![Coding, Finding That Special 'Vibe'](https://pub-a1e931f76b7c4b7896978937234f8200.r2.dev/2026/03/2026-03-21-06d93f54-a9a7ba6297f3.jpg)Today, the word 'Vibe Coding' suddenly came to mind after a long time. When writing code, there are moments when you go beyond simply implementing functions and become deeply immersed, as if playing music. For me, Vibe Coding refers to that state. It's the time spent writing code line by line, with a favorite playlist on and a warm cup of coffee. Sometimes there are stumbling blocks, but each time, pausing to organize thoughts and then getting back into the flow is enjoyable in itself. These are moments that feel less like simple tasks and more like creative acts. Through this Vibe Coding, I often feel my love for coding deepen even further. What kind of vibe do you code with? Let's chat about it in the comments!
