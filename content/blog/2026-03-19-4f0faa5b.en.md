![An Unexpected Taste in Luxembourg: Vietnamese Spring Rolls!](https://pub-a1e931f76b7c4b7896978937234f8200.r2.dev/2026/03/2026-03-19-4f0faa5b-39686ad08281.jpg)Today, I'd like to talk about a special experience I had in Luxembourg. In the heart of beautiful Europe, don't you sometimes suddenly miss the taste of home? I certainly did. After trying European dishes for a few days, I suddenly craved fresh vegetables and a clean sauce. So, I impulsively searched for 'Vietnamese spring rolls'.Surprisingly, I found a cozy yet warm Vietnamese restaurant tucked away in a corner of Luxembourg City! As soon as I opened the door and stepped inside, the familiar yet exotic scent of spices made my heart flutter. The moment the ordered Vietnamese spring rolls were placed on the table, I was amazed by their visual appeal. Large prawns and fresh vegetables were beautifully rolled inside translucent rice paper.With one bite, the harmony of crunchy vegetables, chewy rice paper, and savory peanut sauce was perfect. It almost felt like I was eating at my favorite spot back in Korea. Meeting such familiar and delicious food in an unfamiliar foreign land made the fatigue of travel melt away.A small plate of Vietnamese spring rolls brought me great comfort and joy. If you're traveling in Luxembourg, I highly recommend experiencing this unexpected Vietnamese culinary delight!
