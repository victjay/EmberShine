![Harang's 'Special' Report Card: A Story Filled with Teacher Sonia's Heart](https://pub-a1e931f76b7c4b7896978937234f8200.r2.dev/2026/03/2026-03-18-19088019-b50e66548aea.jpg)One day, a message arrived from Teacher Sonia. Along with the words 'a report card that doesn't feel like Harang's report card,' a photo was attached. My heart pounded as I opened it, wondering what it contained, and a warm story, completely different from the typical report card I had imagined, unfolded before me.The teacher meticulously wrote down Harang's small acts of consideration shown in class, the joyful interactions with friends, and the moments of learning with curious eyes. It fully captured our child's shining qualities, which could never be expressed with numbers or grades. At the last line, 'Harang is always like a small ray of sunshine brightening the surroundings,' my eyes welled up without me realizing it.I was deeply moved by the teacher's heart, which deeply understood the child's individuality and growth beyond simple scores, and viewed them with such warmth. It was truly a grateful and happy day to know that Harang is growing up so loved and healthy. Isn't this kind of heart our child's real 'grade'?
