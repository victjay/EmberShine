Today, I'm going to share a very important tip among Luxembourg travel hacks. It's about 'checking your parking ticket'. Luxembourg is a small country in Europe, but its parking regulations are truly strict. I once almost got into big trouble by letting my guard down for a moment. (Thankfully, I noticed it beforehand! There are probably many people who make the same mistake as me.)Many people enjoy rental car trips in Europe, and it's common to get caught up in the beautiful scenery and fail to properly check their parking tickets or misunderstand parking times. In Luxembourg, this is highly likely to lead to a fine. Especially on weekends, public holidays, and during specific hours, many people carelessly park, thinking it's free, only to find themselves in trouble.**Please remember!**1.  **Check your parking ticket immediately upon issuance:** Verify that the date and time are accurate right away.2.  **Set an alarm for the expiration time:** Setting a phone alarm will help you not forget.3.  **Read parking signs carefully:** Make sure to check whether it's free/paid, time limits, and so on.It's best to avoid unnecessary fines that can detract from the joy of your trip, right? I hope you make many wonderful memories in Luxembourg, and I'll be back with more useful tips next time!
