Facing the weight of a century of time and the diverse stories contained within, it felt truly refreshing. Beyond simply being old, there were many works where one could feel the passion and concerns of that era intact.The attached photo is one of the most impressive works for me, what do you think? I strongly felt that each piece contained the artist's soul. It was a truly meaningful time, commemorating the past while also offering a glimpse of hope for the future.What was particularly memorable was how diverse each artist's interpretation was, even with the same theme. Thanks to this, I was able to immerse myself in each work without realizing how much time had passed. If you ever get the chance to experience such a commemorative exhibition, I highly recommend visiting it. It will be an unforgettable experience. See you in the next post!
